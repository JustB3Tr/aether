# Aether v5 brand
The theme stays graphite with mint teal and a small violet accent. Typography is self-hosted Outfit, distributed under the SIL Open Font License in public/fonts/OFL.txt.

## Logo
Built-in image_gen generated a transparent folded A monogram with an orbital aperture, teal luminous edges, and restrained violet detail.
Prompt direction: premium minimal app icon, folded geometric A made from two glass teal ribbons, crisp silhouette, transparent background, small orbital aperture, graphite-compatible, no text, no mockup.
Original: design/aether-mark.png. Production: public/assets/aether-mark.webp (256px, alpha), favicon.png (64px).
The wordmark is live text, aether., paired with the mark. Do not add text to the icon.

## Home portal
Built-in image_gen generated the home artwork.
Prompt direction: cinematic abstract orbital portal in a graphite glass universe, teal light ribbons with restrained violet accents, floating geometric fragments on the right, dark negative space on the left for a headline, no text, no stock gaming characters.
Original: design/aether-portal.png. Production: public/assets/aether-portal.webp (1600px wide, 110734 bytes).
Production copies were resized/compressed using Pillow; the generated composition was preserved.

## Motion
Page and dialog entrances use opacity/translation, cards use short hover lift and image scale, and loading uses a small orbital indicator. Device reduced-motion and explicit reduced-motion settings stop movement. No continuous hero movement or delayed interaction.
